## 1. Nationwide Government Schemes (Central Programs)

### 1. PM-KISAN (Pradhan Mantri Kisan Samman Nidhi)
- **Official Portal:** [https://pmkisan.gov.in](https://pmkisan.gov.in) & Press Information Bureau ([https://pib.gov.in](https://pib.gov.in))
- **Ministry/Department:** Department of Agriculture & Farmers Welfare, Ministry of Agriculture & Farmers Welfare, Government of India
- **Operational Guidelines:** [PM-KISAN Operational Guidelines](https://pmkisan.gov.in/Documents/Revised_Guidelines_PMKISAN.pdf)
- **Primary Benefit:** Direct cash transfer of ₹6,000 a year, paid as ₹2,000 in three instalments directly into the landholder farmer's Aadhaar-linked bank account via Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT).
- **24th Instalment Release (Access Date 23 November 2026):** PM-KISAN 24th instalment released on 30 October 2026, about 9.34 crore farmers, over ₹18,690 crore transferred. Source: `pmkisan.gov.in` and PIB. Marked *"to be checked against PIB release"*.
- **Key Eligibility Rule:** All cultivable landholding farmer families with land records in the farmer's name in official state revenue records. Requires an Aadhaar-linked bank account and eKYC.
- **Watch Out:** Mismatched or outdated land records can cause exclusion. Check after any land change. Aadhaar seeding must be active on the NPCI DBT mapper.
- **Last Verified On:** 2026-11-23
- **Verified By:** National Agriculture Welfare Desk

### 2. PMFBY (Pradhan Mantri Fasal Bima Yojana)
- **Official Portal:** [https://pmfby.gov.in](https://pmfby.gov.in) & PIB ([https://pib.gov.in](https://pib.gov.in))
- **Ministry/Department:** Department of Agriculture & Farmers Welfare, Government of India
- **Primary Benefit:** Comprehensive crop insurance against notified natural risks (drought, dry spells, flood, pest attack, localized post-harvest losses). Enrolment deadlines are set by each state, so do not show a fixed date.
- **Kharif 2026 Enrolment Progress (Access Date 23 November 2026):** PMFBY Kharif 2026 enrolment progress reported by PIB on 29 August 2026. Source: PIB PMFBY release. Figures to be entered only after checking the PIB release page.
- **Watch Out:** "Check the cut-off date in your state's notification or on pmfby.gov.in." Enrolment deadlines are set individually by each state.
- **Last Verified On:** 2026-11-23
- **Verified By:** National Crop Insurance Review Desk

---

### 3. Kisan Credit Card (KCC) Scheme
- **Official Portal:** [https://agricoop.nic.in](https://agricoop.nic.in) & [https://www.jansamarth.in](https://www.jansamarth.in)
- **Ministry/Department:** Department of Financial Services & Ministry of Agriculture & Farmers Welfare, Government of India
- **Primary Benefit:** Institutional credit facility providing short-term crop loans up to ₹3,00,000 at a concessional interest rate of 4% per annum (with 2% interest subvention and 3% prompt repayment incentive). Working capital up to ₹2,00,000 available for animal husbandry, dairy, and fisheries.
- **Key Eligibility Rule:** Available to all individual farmers, joint borrowers, tenant farmers, oral lessees, sharecroppers, and SHGs/JLGs across all states and union territories.
- **Last Verified On:** 2026-11-23
- **Verified By:** Agricultural Credit Review Cell

---

### 4. Soil Health Card Scheme
- **Official Portal:** [https://soilhealth.dac.gov.in](https://soilhealth.dac.gov.in)
- **Ministry/Department:** Department of Agriculture & Farmers Welfare, Government of India
- **Primary Benefit:** Free comprehensive diagnostic assessment of 12 soil nutrient parameters (N, P, K, S, Zn, Fe, Cu, Mn, Bo, pH, EC, OC) issued cycle-wise every 2 years, with customized crop-specific fertilizer and dosage recommendations.
- **Key Eligibility Rule:** Available to all agricultural landholders across every state and union territory in India.
- **Last Verified On:** 2026-06-15 (More than 90 days ago · Verification status: Review due)
- **Verified By:** Soil Resource & Diagnostics Unit

## 2. Verified State Schemes (Karnataka Baseline)

### 5. Raitha Siri Scheme (ರೈತ ಸಿರಿ ಯೋಜನೆ)
- **Official Portal:** [https://finance.karnataka.gov.in](https://finance.karnataka.gov.in) (Karnataka Budget 2026-27 Document)
- **Department:** Department of Agriculture, Government of Karnataka
- **Primary Benefit:** ₹10,000 per hectare for minor-millet growers (Ragi, Navane, Same, Haraka, Oodalu, Baragu), Agriculture Department, per the Karnataka Budget 2026-27.
- **Unverified Attributes:** Minimum-land rule and direct application portal link are explicitly marked as *"to be verified"*.
- **Last Verified On:** 2026-11-23
- **Verified By:** Karnataka State Agriculture Audit Desk

---

### 6. Krishi Bhagya Scheme (ಕೃಷಿ ಭಾಗ್ಯ ಯೋಜನೆ)
- **Official Portal:** [https://raitamitra.karnataka.gov.in](https://raitamitra.karnataka.gov.in)
- **Department:** Watershed Development & Agriculture Department, Government of Karnataka
- **Primary Benefit:** Subsidised farm ponds and support for rainfed farmers, relaunched in the 2023-24 state budget. Assistance includes farm pond (Krishi Honda) construction and polythene lining.
- **Watch Out:** A pond needs space and a catchment on the farmer's own land.
- **Unverified Attributes:** Specific subsidy amounts and slab percentages are marked as *"to be verified"*.
- **Last Verified On:** 2026-11-23
- **Verified By:** Karnataka State Agriculture Audit Desk

---

### 7. FRUITS Farmer ID (Karnataka Farmer Registry)
- **Official Portal:** [https://fruits.karnataka.gov.in](https://fruits.karnataka.gov.in)
- **Department:** Department of e-Governance & Department of Agriculture, Government of Karnataka
- **Primary Benefit:** One-time registration giving a unique Farmer ID (FID) linked to Bhoomi land data, mandatory for departmental benefits.
- **Unverified Attributes:** Operational verification details and service SLAs marked as *"to be verified"*.
- **Last Verified On:** 2026-11-23
- **Verified By:** Government of Karnataka e-Governance

## 4. Student Project Safety Commitments

- **Zero Personal Data Stored:** No Aadhaar numbers, 16-digit bank account numbers, or land survey numbers are collected, transmitted, or logged.
- **Mandatory Disclaimer:** *"This is an informational guide. It is not an eligibility decision. Confirm with the official scheme office."*
- **Translation Quality Badge:** All Indic translations (Telugu, Kannada, Hindi, Tamil, Malayalam) are transparently labeled *"Translation pending field review"*.
- **Admin Verification Policy:** In Update Centre (`/[locale]/admin/updates`), publishing is blocked unless verified against an official `.gov.in` or `.nic.in` domain, access date is recorded, and change summary is provided.
