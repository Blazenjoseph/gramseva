## 1. Official Scheme Sources

### 1. PM-KISAN (Pradhan Mantri Kisan Samman Nidhi)
- **Official Portal:** [https://pmkisan.gov.in](https://pmkisan.gov.in)
- **Ministry/Department:** Ministry of Agriculture & Farmers Welfare, Government of India
- **Operational Guidelines:** [PM-KISAN Revised Scheme Guidelines](https://pmkisan.gov.in/Documents/Revised_Guidelines_PMKISAN.pdf)
- **Primary Benefit:** Direct cash transfer of ₹6,000 per year in 3 equal instalments of ₹2,000 via DBT (Direct Benefit Transfer) into Aadhaar-seeded bank accounts.
- **Key Eligibility Rule:** Landholder farmer families having cultivable landholding in their names. Excludes institutional landholders, income tax payers, serving/retired government officers, and pension recipients above ₹10,000/month.
- **Mandatory Requirements:** Land record (RoR/RTC), Aadhaar number linked to active bank account (NPCI DBT Aadhaar seeding), eKYC completion (OTP or biometric).
- **Last Verified On:** 2026-11-15
- **Verified By:** Group 5 Research Lead, JAIN University
- **Verification Note:** Farmers with joint land records (common in Karnataka) can receive benefits proportionate to their documented ownership, provided name matches RTC and Aadhaar.

### 2. Raitha Siri Scheme (రೈತ ಸಿರಿ ಯೋಜನೆ)
- **Official Portal:** [https://raitamitra.karnataka.gov.in](https://raitamitra.karnataka.gov.in)
- **Department:** Department of Agriculture, Government of Karnataka
- **Government Order (G.O.):** Agriculture Department G.O. No. AGRI/28/AGS/2019
- **Primary Benefit:** Financial incentive of ₹10,000 per hectare (up to a maximum of 2 hectares, i.e., ₹20,000) for farmers growing minor millets (Ragi, Foxtail millet/Navane, Little millet/Same, Kodo millet/Haraka, Barnyard millet/Oodalu, Proso millet/Baragu).
- **Mandatory Requirements:** FRUITS Farmer ID (FID), RTC (Pahani) reflecting minor millet cultivation verified via Karnataka Farmer Crop Survey (Bele Samikshe), Aadhaar-linked bank account.
- **Target Persona Alignment:** Directly supports small and marginal ragi farmers (e.g., half-acre cultivator) in southern and central dry zones of Karnataka.
- **Last Verified On:** 2026-11-15
- **Verified By:** Group 5 Agriculture Policy Desk, JAIN University
- **Verification Note:** Land parcel must be registered in the FRUITS database with verified crop survey data for the kharif/rabi season before DBT disbursal.

### 3. Krishi Bhagya Scheme (ಕೃಷಿ ಭಾಗ್ಯ ಯೋಜನೆ)
- **Official Portal:** [https://raitamitra.karnataka.gov.in](https://raitamitra.karnataka.gov.in)
- **Department:** Department of Agriculture, Watershed Development Department, Government of Karnataka
- **Primary Benefit:** Sustainable dryland farming package: financial assistance of up to 80% to 90% (for SC/ST farmers) and up to 50% (for general/OBC farmers) for constructing farm ponds (Krishi Honda), polythene lining to prevent percolation, diesel/solar pump sets, micro-irrigation (drip/sprinkler), and shade nets.
- **Mandatory Requirements:** Minimum 1 acre (or 0.5 acre for horticultural/dryland clusters with joint community farm ponds), RTC/Pahani showing dryland/rainfed agricultural land, FRUITS ID, Bank passbook copy, Caste certificate (if seeking affirmative action subsidy bracket).
- **Where to Apply:** Raitha Samparka Kendra (RSK) at the Hobli level, or Assistant Director of Agriculture (ADA) taluk office.
- **Last Verified On:** 2026-11-16
- **Verified By:** Group 5 Field Studies Unit, JAIN University
- **Verification Note:** Highly dependent on annual budget allocations and target allotments per hobli. Applications open seasonally prior to the south-west monsoon.

### 4. FRUITS Farmer ID (Farmer Registration and Unified Beneficiary Information System)
- **Official Portal:** [https://fruits.karnataka.gov.in](https://fruits.karnataka.gov.in)
- **Department:** e-Governance Department & Department of Agriculture, Government of Karnataka
- **Primary Purpose:** Unified single-window farmer registry linking Aadhaar, Bhoomi Land Records (RTC/Pahani), bank accounts, and caste certificates into an immutable 8 or 9-digit Farmer Identification Number (FID).
- **Why It Is Essential:** Mandatory prerequisite for accessing over 14 departmental schemes in Karnataka (Agriculture, Horticulture, Sericulture, Animal Husbandry, Fisheries, Social Forestry).
- **Where to Register:** Grama One centers, Bangalore One centers, Karnataka One centers, or Hobli-level Raitha Samparka Kendra (RSK).
- **Last Verified On:** 2026-11-15
- **Verified By:** Group 5 Data & Technology Team, JAIN University

---

### 5. PM Kisan Maandhan Yojana (PM-KMY) & PM Fasal Bima Yojana (PMFBY)
- **Official PMFBY Portal:** [https://pmfby.gov.in](https://pmfby.gov.in)
- **Official PM-KMY Portal:** [https://maandhan.in](https://maandhan.in)
- **Primary Purpose:** Weather-based and yield-based crop insurance coverage for notifiable crops (such as ragi, groundnut, pulses) and old-age social security pension (₹3,000/month after age 60) for small and marginal farmers owning up to 2 hectares.
- **Last Verified On:** 2026-11-14
- **Verified By:** Group 5 Research Lead, JAIN University

## 2. Terminology & Regulatory Explanations

1. **Aadhaar e-KYC & NPCI DBT Seeding:**
   - Reference: [National Payments Corporation of India (NPCI) Bharat Aadhaar Seeding Enabler](https://www.npci.org.in)
   - Critical Clarification: Linking Aadhaar to a bank account for OTP/authentication is *different* from seeding Aadhaar on the NPCI mapper for DBT transfers. A farmer can have Aadhaar in three bank accounts, but DBT can only flow into the single NPCI-mapped account.
2. **Bhoomi RTC (Record of Rights, Tenancy and Crops / Pahani):**
   - Reference: [Karnataka Bhoomi Land Records](https://landrecords.karnataka.gov.in)
   - Field 9 (Owner details) and Field 12 (Crop details) are scanned by state automated verification algorithms.
3. **Bele Samikshe (Farmer Mobile Crop Survey):**
   - Reference: [Karnataka State Remote Sensing Applications Centre (KSRSAC) & Agriculture Dept](https://cropsurvey.karnataka.gov.in)
   - Self-uploaded or enumerator-uploaded geo-tagged photos of standing crops determining subsidy validity.

## 4. Student Project Disclaimer & Safety Commitments

- **No Personal Identification Numbers Collected:** Scheme Access Kit never solicits, stores, transmits, or logs Aadhaar numbers, 16-digit bank account numbers, IFSC codes, or land survey numbers.
- **Readiness vs. Official Decision:** All questionnaire outcomes are explicitly labeled: *"This is an informational guidance tool, not a legal or administrative eligibility decision. Confirm with the official scheme office."*
- **Translation Status:** Indic script translations (Kannada, Hindi, Tamil, Malayalam) are initially generated with human review for key agricultural terms, and systematically tagged with a visible *"Community translated / pending field reviewer sign-off"* badge for complete transparency.
